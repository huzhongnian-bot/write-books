# 《窄门》定妆照提示词

> 2026-09-13 依据设定档第四节/第八节 + 两张参考图整理。Jacob 参考图为蓝调黑色漫画风（藏青 T 恤、砖墙、顶光）；Jack 参考图为手绘速写风（白衬衫、松垮领带、胡茬、丧脸）。两套提示词分别对应两种画风，也可互换。

---

## Jacob（32，本名 Jacobo）

**参考图风格还原版（蓝调黑色漫画）**

```
Portrait of a tall, athletic Latino man in his early 30s, dark brown skin, short
black curly hair, light beard stubble along a sharp jawline, high brow bones,
deep brown eyes that weigh people like prices — alert, guarded, a hint of a
smirk lifting the left corner of his mouth first. Wearing a plain dark navy
t-shirt stretched over a muscular chest, arms resting casually. Old faded tattoo
on his forearm. Clean nails, expensive watch barely visible — details that
betray money under the sloppy clothes. Standing against a rough dark brick wall,
single cold overhead light, deep blue monochrome palette, noir webtoon /
manhwa comic style, heavy ink shadows, gritty texture.
```

**设定档定妆方向版（暖光暗调，突出"衣柜里的小孩"）**

```
Character portrait, Latino man early 30s, dark skin, black short curls, beard,
warm dim amber lighting against deep shadow. Wearing a loose black sweater,
slouchy posture, eyes wary and defensive — the look of someone who once hid in
a closet, not a hunter's ease. Handsome but weathered, left corner of mouth
curls up first when he smiles. Painterly semi-realistic style, cinematic rim
light, muted warm tones, shallow depth, film-noir mood.
```

**画面要点（给画师/调图用）**
- 笑时**左边嘴角先翘**（设定档明确特征，别画成对称笑）
- 穿着散漫（卫衣/工装夹克/旧球鞋），但表不便宜、指甲干净、牙太白——"露馅感"是定妆的一部分
- 眼神要警觉防备，不是狩猎者的从容
- 肌肉是"街区求生本能 + 私教课抛光"，精壮不是健美

## Jack（30）

**参考图风格还原版（手绘速写）**

```
Loose pencil-sketch portrait of a worn-out American white-collar worker around
30, white male, messy dark-brown hair still thick (his only remaining asset),
heavy dark circles under half-lidded eyes, light stubble, face blank with
resignation — the maximum emotional expression is "oh". Wrinkled white dress
shirt with rolled sleeves, loose dark-olive tie askew, cheap belt. Slight
slouch, shoulders caved in from years at a desk. Small wavy stress lines
floating above his head. Rough hand-drawn sketch style, minimal flat color,
white background, quiet deadpan comedy mood.
```

**同一角色的彩图版**

```
Character portrait, ordinary American office worker, 30, forgettable face that
vanishes in a subway crowd in three seconds. Dark brown hair, gray-green tired
eyes, stubble, blue-black shadows under eyes from chronic sleep debt. Uniqlo-
style plain shirt, tie loosened. Holding a cheap convenience-store coffee.
Fluorescent office lighting, muted desaturated palette, slice-of-life anime /
webtoon style, aura of a man whose life philosophy is "whatever, too much
trouble".
```

**画面要点**
- 关键词是**普**：扔进人堆三秒消失，不能画帅，帅了就崩设定（建模豁免权是 Jacob 的，不是他的）
- 但头发要茂盛（设定档原话"唯一的资产"）
- 表情上限"哦"：丧、蔫、自嘲，不是忧郁美型
- 衣柜全是优衣库同款不同色——服装参考按这个走

## 合照（可选）

```
Two-shot character sheet: a tall muscular Latino man in a navy tee leaning
casually against a kitchen door frame, eating leftovers with zero shame,
smirking (left corner of mouth first); next to him a rumpled white office
worker hunched over a cracked laptop, tie loose, dark circles, completely
unbothered by the intruder. Cheap apartment interior — marble-lobby building
but the unit is the poor one: old furniture, instant-noodle cups, a bedsheet
for a curtain. Warm tungsten lamp vs cold night window, noir-comedy tone.
```
